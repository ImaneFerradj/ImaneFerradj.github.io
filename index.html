Soft & Continuum Robotics · Smart-Material Actuation

Researching motion

I’m Imane Ferradj, a PhD candidate in Robotics & Automation at Arts et Métiers, working on electroactive-polymer-actuated continuum robots, nonlinear control, and minimally invasive medical robotics.

Detailed profile Publications

Research perspective

From material response
to robotic behaviour.

My work spans smart materials, actuator characterization, continuum mechanics, hysteresis compensation, model predictive control, and the design of robotic systems for constrained environments.

Current research

Electroactive continuum robots.

Multiscale models for smart-material-actuated continuum robots, from electroactive polymer behaviour to soft robotic deformation.

Control

Curvature, hysteresis & MPC.

Model-based control of nonlinear soft actuators, including distributed MPC.

Application

Minimally invasive robotics.

Compact continuum robotic systems designed for highly constrained environments.

Research path

Paris → Daejeon → Grenoble → Marseille → Paris.

My work has moved across electrical engineering, soft robotics, continuum mechanics, bio-inspired flight and smart-material actuation.
